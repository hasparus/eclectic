Variations

Black is material, white is cut away. The stencil file is a single even-odd path; the cut file is the same outline as red 0.1 mm hairlines, the usual cut layer in laser software. Loose pieces are removed and openings smaller than 0.6 mm² are filled, so nothing falls out of the sheet.
